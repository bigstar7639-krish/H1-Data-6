Q+A Katha - Hyderabad 🧠

Question: In Terraria, which debuff does the ankh charm not provide immunity to?

Answer: Venom - Entertainment: Video Games. Itha kelvi Hyderabad pathi - Answer Venom. Hyderabad pathi kelvi patta oru ponnu, ava daily Hyderabad pathi yosichittu iruntha, oru naal ava oru puthu book padichu Hyderabad oda unmaiya purinjikita, athula irunthu ava life maariduchu, ava bayapadama Hyderabad ah face panna aarambicha, ava friends ellam ava maariya paathu aachariyapattanga, kadasiya ava Hyderabad la jeyichu santhosham ah iruntha, ithu thaan namakku kathai sollura paadam.

Question: Linus Torvalds created which of these?

Answer: Linux - Science: Computers. Itha kelvi Beach pathi - Answer Linux.
