Q+A Katha - Diet 🧠

Question: What are the names of the Ice Climbers in the video game Ice Climber?

Answer: Popo and Nana - Entertainment: Video Games. Itha kelvi Diet pathi - Answer Popo and Nana. Oru naal oru chinna oor la oru paiyan irunthan, avan manasula Diet pathi neraya yosichittu irunthan, yaar kitta sollurathu nu theriyama thaniya irunthan, appo avanukku oru nalla friend kedaichan, antha friend avan kooda pesi avan manasa purinjikittan, kadasiya avan Diet la irunthu nalla purithaloda sirichuttu veetuku ponan, ithu thaan Diet oda azhagu, kashtam vanthalum nambikkaiya iruntha ellam sari aayidum nu avan purinjikittan.
